# Flashcards

## What does SIEM stand for?

Security Information and Event Management.

---

## What is SPL?

Splunk's Search Processing Language.

---

## What is an index?

A place where Splunk organizes data for searching.

---

## What does `|` do in SPL?

It passes the results of one command to another command.

---

## What does `stats` do?

It calculates statistics from search results.

---

## What is event correlation?

Connecting related events to understand a bigger pattern.

---

## What is a false positive?

Legitimate activity incorrectly identified as suspicious.

---

## What is alert tuning?

Adjusting detection logic to reduce unnecessary alerts without losing important visibility.

---

## Do repeated failed logins always mean an attack?

No. Context is required.

---

## Why does time matter?

The same number of events can have very different meanings depending on how quickly they happen.

---

## Quick reminder

```text
SIEM → Centralize and correlate

SPL  → Search and analyze

SOC  → Detect, investigate, respond
```
